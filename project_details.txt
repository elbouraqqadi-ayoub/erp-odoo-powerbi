End-to-End ERP System for Revenue Management & Business Intelligence: 

Odoo 17, Python, PostgreSQL, Power BI, ETL, Linux

• Developed and deployed a custom Odoo ERP extension module in Python, optimizing financial workflows and
enterprise resource visibility.
• Modeled relational schemas in PostgreSQL on Linux and established secure data synchronization layers to support
complex analytics workflows.
• Architected a reliable ETL pipeline utilizing ODBC protocols to extract multi-departmental transactional data
seamlessly.
• Designed interactive dashboards and analytical models in Power BI to monitor real-time financial KPIs and revenue
performance metrics.
